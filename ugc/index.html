Summary of what you'll do in this guide
  • Create a new Instagram and TikTok account
  • Warm up period of 2 days
  • Learn when to post
  • Learn how to reply to comments
Time required
  • Setup: ~30 minutes
  • Daily posting: ~30 seconds

Step 2 Account warm up (🚨 important)
Day 1
  • Spend 20–30 minutes engaging with budgeting, finance and iPhone content before posting your first video.
  • Watch videos until the end
  • Like content in your exact niche — iPhone tricks, budgeting, money, finance, Apple
  • Follow 20 relevant accounts max (not all on day 1, do 5 per day)
  • DO NOT post anything on day 1
Day 2
  • Like some videos
  • Comment on some videos
  • You want your algorithm to think you’re the user type you’re trying to target.
Step 3 Pin the set up video
  1. Download Set Up video

  2. Post the Set Up Video.
  3. Include a relevant caption for the video and tag @quanto.tracker
  4. Pin the video to the top of the profile.
Step 4 Post the video
  1. Copy a caption for your video from quanto.app/captions:

  2. Post one video per day on both Instagram and TikTok.
  3. Post between 4 PM and 8 PM.

Step 6 · IGNORE — only for users recording videos How to record the videos
  1. Open Quanto on the Activity page.
  2. Swipe on the main chart until you reach the month that shows all the transactions.
  3. While Quanto is open, lock the iPhone.
  4. Before filming, unlock the iPhone once off camera so you do not need to unlock it during the video.
  5. With the second phone, start recording.
  6. Show the object you are about to track for at least 1 second.
  7. Bring the iPhone into the frame.
  8. Turn the iPhone around to show the back of the phone.
  9. Double tap the back of the iPhone.
  10. Turn the iPhone to the front to show that the double tap shortcut was activated.
  11. Input the amount.
  12. Select a category that matches the object shown in the video.
  13. Add a description if needed.
  14. Tap Done.
  15. Unlock the phone to show the Quanto Activity page.
  16. Tap through a few different pages and filters to show more of the app.
