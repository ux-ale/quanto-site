9AI features

Quanto’s AI features are optional. Nothing described in this section happens unless you choose to use one of them.

a) What is shared

When you choose to use an AI feature:

  • Voice Transactions: Your device turns your speech into text. Quanto does not receive or store your voice recording. The text, selected currency, and your category and account names are used to create transaction suggestions.
  • Receipt Scanning: A smaller copy of the receipt image is sent for processing. Location and other hidden image information are removed first.
  • PDF Scanning: Your original PDF is not uploaded. Quanto sends text taken from the document or, when needed, a smaller image of a page. PDF passwords stay on your device and are never sent or stored.
  • Apple Pay Automation: If you set up the optional Apple Shortcuts automation, payment details are saved to Quanto on your device. Most payments are categorized on your device. When Quanto cannot choose a category, only the merchant name and your expense category names are sent for help. The amount, date, card name, and account are not sent.

Quanto does not connect directly to Apple Pay, your bank, or your card provider. It receives Apple Pay details only through the automation you create.

b) How your information is handled

Google Gemini processes the information needed to provide these features. Information sent to Google Gemini through Quanto’s paid service is not used to train Google’s AI models.

Your information is encrypted while being sent. Quanto does not sell it or use it for advertising or tracking.

Quanto does not keep voice recordings, voice transcripts, receipt or PDF content, PDF passwords, or Apple Pay merchant names on its servers. Limited technical records are kept to protect the service, fix problems, and apply feature usage limits. Most of these records are deleted within 90 days, while usage totals may be kept for as long as they are needed to apply those limits.

c) AI results and your choices

AI can make mistakes. Voice, receipt, and PDF results are shown as editable drafts and are saved only after you confirm them.

Apple Pay transactions are saved automatically after you set up the automation, but you can edit them at any time. Correcting a category helps Quanto categorize the same merchant better on your device in the future.

Quanto is an expenses management tool and does not provide financial, accounting, or tax advice. Check important information against the original receipt or document.

You can stop using these features at any time. You can also remove camera, photo, microphone, or speech permissions in your device settings. To stop Apple Pay Automation, delete the Quanto automation from Apple’s Shortcuts app.
